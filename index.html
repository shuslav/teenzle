This policy explains what Teenzle does with data.

What the game itself collects

Nothing. Teenzle stores your progress, settings, and purchase state on your device only. That data never leaves the device, is never sent to us, and we have no way to read it. There is no account, no login, no cloud save, and no analytics of any kind. Delete the app and the data goes with it.

We do not know who you are, how far you got, or that you play at all.

Advertising

Teenzle shows ads through Google AdMob, a Google service. Ads appear in two places: between levels, and before the in-game hint. Players who purchase “Remove ads” see none.

To choose and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and processes data on its own behalf, not ours. This typically includes:

  • the device advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID on Android, IDFA on iOS);
  • IP address, which reveals approximate location — normally the city, not the street;
  • device and app information: model, operating system version, language, app version;
  • interaction with the ad itself: that it was shown, viewed, or tapped.

We receive none of this in a form that identifies you. Our AdMob account shows only totals — how many ads were shown, how much they earned.

Google's handling of this data is governed by Google's own terms:

  • Privacy Policy — https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from partner sites and apps — https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Your choice about personalised ads

Ads can be personalised — chosen using data about you gathered across apps and sites — or non-personalised, chosen only from the current context.

Where the law requires it, including the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the game asks before any personalised ad is shown, using Google's User Messaging Platform. Your answer is remembered. You can change it at any time: the game's Settings screen offers “Privacy options”, which reopens the same dialog.

Declining costs you nothing. The game stays complete: no level, hint, or feature is withheld. You will still see ads, but non-personalised ones.

On iOS the system additionally asks for permission to track before any tracking occurs. That prompt is Apple's, and declining it is final — the game does not ask again.

Purchases

Teenzle offers one optional purchase: Remove ads. It is handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store; we never see or store your payment details.

The store tells the game one thing: whether you own the purchase. The game asks that question each time it starts, which is also how the purchase reaches your other devices signed in to the same store account.

The purchase belongs to the store account, not to the device — and not to both platforms: buying on Android does not remove ads on iOS, because they are separate stores with separate products. We cannot link them, because we have no accounts of our own.

Data retention

We retain nothing, because we receive nothing. Data collected by Google is retained under Google's policies, linked above.

Children

Teenzle is not directed at children under 13, or such higher age as your law requires. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The game is declared to AdMob as not child-directed, and ads shown in it are rated accordingly.

If you believe a child has provided personal information through this app, please write to us and we will act on it.

Your rights

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the GDPR gives you the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal data, and to object to its processing. If you are a California resident, the CCPA gives you the right to know what personal information is collected and to ask that it not be sold or shared.

Because we hold no personal data about you, we have nothing to disclose or delete on request. For data held by Google, exercise these rights through Google, whose policy is linked above. Two controls are always in your hands: reset or delete your device advertising identifier in system settings, and reopen the consent dialog from the game's Settings screen.

Security

The game keeps no personal data on our side, so there is nothing on our side to breach. Progress and settings on your device are protected by your device's own security.

Data breach notification

Should anything change and a breach ever affect data we hold, we will publish notice on this page and, where the law requires it, notify the relevant authority within the statutory period.

Changes

This policy may change as the game changes. Material changes will be published on this page with a new “Last updated” date. Continuing to use the game after that constitutes acceptance.
